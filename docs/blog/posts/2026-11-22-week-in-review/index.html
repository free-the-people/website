Alaska Senate race results are in: Peltola brings Democrats to 56-44 majority [Link]

Mary Peltola, 2022.

The results are finally in, and Mary Peltola has officially defeated two-term Republican Senator Dan Sullivan in Alaska. This race was the last Senate race to be called, and brings the next Senate's composition to 56 Democrats and 44 Republicans.

Oh, and that lawsuit alleging that Peltola had recruited a second Dan Sullivan to run against the incumbent Republican Dan Sullivan? Well, the Trump regime is dropping that meritless lawsuit that would never have held up in court anyway.

Putin, Xi, and Trump. 2026 meeting of the Axis of Evil.

Lame-duck Senate Republicans likely to approve Trump-aligned candidate for Comptroller General [Link]

Eric Ueland, 2018.

As January approaches, the lame-duck Republican-led Senate is considering rubber-stamping a new candidate for Comptroller General. Eric Ueland, a long-time Trump ally who currently serves in the Office of Management and Budget (OMB). The OMB is actively working to cancel federal funding that has already been approved by Congress.

The Comptroller General is responsible for auditing federal spending on behalf of Congress. So the choice of a person who is actively working to subvert the power of the purse that is explicitly and exclusively granted to the Congress by the United States Constitution to a 15-year term in an office designed for auditing federal spending is absolutely ridiculous.

Trump's DOJ is actively recruiting unqualified candidates [Link]

Modified from image by Pete Linforth from Pixabay.

Traditionally, lawyers wouldn't even be considered for a position as an assistant U.S. attorney without at least three years of experience after passing the bar. But that's changed under the Trump regime.

Today, inexperienced law-school graduates are being fast-tracked to fill the places of the career prosecutors who were fired or fled under Trump's weaponization of the DOJ. Traditionally, the DOJ prosecuted crimes like fraud and drugs, but all that has changed as Trump wants to focus almost exclusively on prosecuting immigrants and his own political enemies.

The fact that the DOJ is hiring, and indeed actively recruiting, right out of law school — it’s an astounding break with past practice.

It reflects a lowering of standards for a job that is important and requires lawyers who are meticulous, committed to doing the right thing, and bring with them experience beyond just law school.

US loses measles-free status as MAHA fear-mongering has led gullible Americans to forgo vaccination [Link]

CDC chart of US measles cases since 2000.

This past year, the United States saw the highest rate in measles infections in more than 30 years. As a result, the country has lost is status as measles-free, which it has held for decades now.

While there are no real practical implications of this loss, it serves as a reminder of the dumbing-down of the American public regarding the importance of vaccination. Vaccination rates have been falling for several diseases in the U.S. over the past few years as anti-science conspiracy theories have gained traction.

U.S. Health Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr. has been a leading voice in the anti-vaccine movement for years before he was selected for the position. He has offered conflicting advice on measles vaccinations over the years, falsely claiming that measles can be treated by vitamin A and cod liver oil and that the measles vaccine can be fatal.
