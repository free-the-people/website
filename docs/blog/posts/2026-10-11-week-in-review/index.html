I wouldn't vote for this regime for a billion dollars [Link]

Image by S K from Pixabay.

The Republican Party is prepared to spend up to a billion dollars to try to convince voters to support their lost cause, but even that may not be enough to overcome the stunning unpopularity of Donny 2-Dolls and the disaster he's made of this country.

And because these candidates started their campaigns under the assumption that their district was safely Republican, they didn't reserve enough ad time early on, and are now paying more for the same ad spots their opponents reserved early on at deep discounts.

ICE agents disguised as construction workers shoot a man in his car with a 5-year-old boy inside [Link]

Image by Clker-Free-Vector-Images from Pixabay.

This week, ICE agents shot a man in his car with a 5-year-old boy inside. The agents fired at least seven shots at the car, and miraculously, the boy was unharmed. The agents were disguised as construction workers when they approached the vehicle while the driver, Oscar Belgal, was parking. As they approached the vehicle, Belgal started to pull forward and at least one agent drew his weapon and fired seven shots at the vehicle.

Sadly, it's hardly even news anymore when ICE agents gun people down in our streets. But what makes this case particularly disturbing is the brazen nature of the deception. These agents actively disguised themselves so they wouldn't be identifiable as federal agents, then shot someone for refusing to stop his car when they pointed a gun at him. To make matters worse, the agents did not even care that there was a child in the vehicle while they were discharging firearms at it.

If you're inclined to dismiss the agents' behavior by saying, "Well, maybe they didn't know there was a child in the car," that doesn't make this any better. In fact, it just proves how reckless these gunmen are. They draw their guns and fire at a vehicle either knowingly endangering the life of a child, or without knowing who else might be inside the vehicle they are shooting at.

What can you do this week?

Check your voter registration [Link]

National Association of Secretaries of State

Check your voter registration online. The National Association of Secretaries of State is a good place to start, and will direct you to the appropriate state voter registration website.

If you aren't registered, register today so you can vote in the midterms and beyond. If your are registered, start preparing for the elections. Check your ballot, research the candidates and issues you will be voting on. Decide how your are going to vote (by mail, by drop box, in person, on election day, during early voting periods, etc.).

And don't forget to sign up for and attend a No Kings rally near you to show the world you do not support tyranny.
