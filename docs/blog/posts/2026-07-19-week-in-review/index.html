Fact-checking Trump's prime-time lies [Link]

Donny 2-Dolls, a national disgrace

This week, Donny 2-Dolls demanded networks across the country air his prime-time address to the nation in which he reiterated his false claims that the 2020 election was stolen and that voter fraud is a widespread problem in U.S. elections. He also spent a significant amount of time posing hypothetical threats and offered no evidence that these made-up problems are actually affecting U.S. elections. The only claim he made that has any basis in truth is his claim that foreign powers could hack into election systems to change votes. That one actually happened, but in Venezuela, not in the United States.

The whole point of Trump's insane public rantings is to try to create a sense of urgency around the SAVE America Act, which he is hoping Congress will pass to disenfranchise millions of legitimate voters who are likely to vote for Democrats instead of Republicans. One of the most prominent features of the SAVE act is that it would make it much more difficult for married women (who have changed their name to their husband's family name) to register to vote. This is in line with the Trump regime's push to stop women from voting by restricting voting to just one vote per household instead of one vote per person.

The most notable part of the speech was that Trump did not offer any solutions to any of the problems that he mentioned. Furthermore, despite his repeated claims of fraud, he has not prosecuted anyone for election-related offenses, which he would surely do if he had any proof whatsoever for his insane claims.

Trump removes members of the Election Assistance Commission [Link]

Seal of the Election Assistance Commission.

Donny 2-Dolls has removed several board members of the Election Assistance Commission, a move that has raised concerns about the integrity of the U.S. electoral process. The commission, which provides technical support for elections, has been a target of Trump's criticism, and his removal of key members signals a continued effort to reshape the way elections are conducted in the United States.

This is the first major test of a new Supreme Court ruling allowing the president to fire members of independent agencies without cause. These EAC members were fired because they resisted his efforts to make the voter registration process more difficult. The whole point of having independent agencies in the government is to provide a check on the power wielded by the executive branch. Which means that these people were fired for doing their jobs well. Technically, he only fired 2 of the 4 members of the board...one resigned and another had left his post earlier this year. The EAC is responsible for distributing grants to state and local election offices, and now that it has no board members, it may not be able to fulfill its mission.

Purging commissioners just months before the midterm elections and further gutting support for our state and local elections officials is a blatant part of his plan to politicize our elections and enable more unlawful and dangerous election interference.

Drained reflecting pool reveals shoddy workmanship instead of evidence of vandalism [Link]

Drained reflecting pool shows a gray (not blue) surface covered in what appear to be tire tracks from the motorcade Trump drove across the pool while the liner was drying.

The Reflecting Pool in Washington D.C. is turning into a perfect analogy for Donny 2-Dolls' legacy of bad business deals, blatant corruption, and inability to do anything right. After promising to beautify the Reflecting Pool for the low, low cost of $1.5 million, he ended up giving a no-bid contract to his personal choice of a "guy who's unbelievable at doing swimming pools," resulting in a cost that's already ballooned to around $16 million—over ten times the budgeted cost. And we're not done yet, because that doesn't include the latest efforts to drain the Reflecting Pool (again) and repair the "damage" (again).

Trump has repeatedly claimed, without a shred of evidence, that the latest round of problems with the Reflecting Pool were the result of vandals cutting a 350-foot long gash into the liner of the pool. Now that the pool has been drained, everyone can see that there is no tear or gash visible anywhere in the pool's surface. But the original shoddy workmanship is on display for all to see. We can also see that the paint has faded from what Trump called "American flag blue" has faded to gray, even though the liner has only been in place for a matter of weeks, despite his claims that the liner would last for 50 to 100 years.

Detainees at Texas ICE facility report human rights abuse including frequent beatings [Link]

Camp East Montana

More than 90% of those interviewed about their treatment at a Texas ICE facility called Camp East Montana report being beaten or witnessing other detainees being beaten. They also report a wide variety of other human rights violations, including being denied necessary medical care, given inedible food, forced to live in inhumane conditions, and collective punishment (A practice where guards beat multiple people after accusing one detainee of breaking a rule).

The abuses documented are consistent with reports from other ICE facilities around the country. At least three people have died at the facility, including one who was handcuffed and pinned by guards until he stopped breathing. The death was ruled a homicide by the medical examiner, and evidence in the case was "missing or destroyed." The facility is one of many inhumane concentration camps currently in operation on American soil, and ICE is rapidly building new detention facilities out of old warehouses.

Lindsey Graham dies unexpectedly [Link]

Lindsey Graham and Donny 2-Dolls.

Senator Lindsey Graham died unexpectedly earlier this week after a "brief and sudden illness." Later examination showed the cause of death to be a tear in his aorta related to hardening of his arteries. Graham is best known for his total reversal of positions on Trump. In 2015, he summarized his views of Trump by saying, "He's a race-baiting, xenophobic, religious bigot. He doesn't represent my party." By 2026, his assessment of Trump had changed to, "Mr. President, you're not far behind God." Such a complete reversal of stance is more than just changing positions on a subject when new facts emerge. Graham was once one of the most vocal opponents of Trump, but as soon as Trump won the 2016 election, Graham spent the next decade worming his way into the president's good graces with sycophantic expressions of flattery in order to protect his own career as a Senator at the expense of his consituents and the American people. Graham briefly tried to disassociate himself from Trump after the January 6th insurrection, but as soon as he saw that Trump would not be held accountable for his participation in the assault on the capital, he went right back to sucking up to Trump.

Graham was a hawk when it came to foreign policy, cheering on Israel's genocide in Gaza and celebrating Trump's war with Iran. Graham was instrumental in bypassing the normal legislative process to push through controversial legislation to pass significant Republican policies without allowing the Democrats to filibuster. He did break with some of the Party's positions by arguing for the need for a valid path to citizenship for immigrants. He also supported Ukraine's efforts to defend itself against Russian aggression, leading some to suspect that Graham's death might have been an assassination by Russian agents. Graham's Senate seat will be filled by his sister, Darline Graham, who was appointed to fill the position by the governor of South Carolina in accordance with state law. Darline has no political experience, having worked as an optician and having received a master's degree in rehabilitation counseling. A special election will be held next month to find a replacement for Graham on the November 2026 ballot to take over his seat in January.
