The good...

ABC suing FCC for politically-motivated censorship/free speech violations [Link]

The FCC has threatened to strip local ABC stations of their broadcast licenses because the Trump regime doesn't like some of the content broadcast by Disney-owned ABC stations, especially commentary on the daytime talk show The View.

The purpose of the licenses issued to broadcasters is to ensure that they use the public airways they control in the public interest. Traditionally, that has been understood to mean that these broadcasters were serving their local communities by sharing information about weather, sports, and news.

Shows like The View provide commentary on current events that is understood to be protected by the First Amendment right to free speech, and is perfectly acceptable under FCC regulations. It's worth noting that similar opinion-sharing shows that support the current regime are not being threatened with loss of their licenses.
