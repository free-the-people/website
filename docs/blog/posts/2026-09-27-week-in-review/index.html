Trump regime's EPA illegally terminated solar program aimed at helping poor Americans [Link]

Image by Leopictures from Pixabay.

The Trump regime recently terminated a $7 billion program named "Solar for All" that aimed at making solar power accessible to hundreds of thousands of lower-income Americans. The program awarded grants to states, tribes, and regions to invest in rooftop solar and community solar gardens in an attempt to introduce clean power into the electrical grid and lower the cost of electricity.

The program was administered by the EPA and was authorized and funded by Congress. The EPA terminated the program without any authority and against the will of Congress, which is illegal.

This is just one of many programs aimed at lowering greenhouse gasses that has been canceled by the Trump regime in its fight against clean energy, lower costs for consumers, and the creation of high-quality union jobs—which are exactly the things the American people want the most right now.

Supreme Court allows Trump regime to vet voter citizenship using flawed database [Link]

Image by andreas160578 from Pixabay.

The Supreme Court issued an emergency ruling that allows the Trump regime to use a highly controversial database that is known to be full of bad data to remove voter registration for anyone alleged to be a non-citizen.

The Systematic Alien Verification for Entitlements (SAVE) database that will be used to "verify" citizenship status of registered voters has a long and well-documented history of producing false positives. If this data is used to automatically remove voter registrations, it will de-register legal voters. The system will run automatically, meaning voters will not be given notice that their voter registration has been removed, which will prevent them from voting in future elections.

The change will not impact the current mid-term election cycle, as federal law prohibits the government from automatically updating voter registration within 90 days of an election. But this will certainly have an impact on the 2028 presidential election.

The Supreme Court ruling reverses a lower court ruling to block the Trump regime from using the SAVE database filed in June. The judge in that case had this to say:

The federal government has knowingly trampled on the privacy rights of American citizens in a manner that threatens the sacred right to vote.

This Court cannot stand idly by while that happens.
